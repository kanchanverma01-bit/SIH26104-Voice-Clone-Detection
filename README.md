# SIH26104-Voice-Clone-Detection
# 🎙️ AI-Powered Real-Time Detection and Prevention of Voice Cloning Impersonation Attacks

> **Smart India Hackathon 2026 | SIH26104**

An AI-powered security system designed to detect and prevent **voice-cloning and synthetic voice impersonation attacks** in real time.

The system combines **AI-generated voice detection**, **speaker verification**, **risk assessment**, and **automated prevention mechanisms** to identify suspicious voice interactions and protect users from voice-based impersonation attacks.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Advances in generative AI have made it possible to clone a person's voice using only a small amount of voice data.

Attackers can exploit this technology to impersonate:

- 👤 CEOs and senior executives
- 🏦 Bank customers and account holders
- 💼 Employees and organizational authorities
- 📞 Family members or known individuals
- 🔐 Authorized users in voice-based authentication systems

Traditional voice authentication systems often focus primarily on **speaker identity**.

However, a cloned voice may sound highly similar to the target speaker while being generated by an AI model.

Therefore, a secure system must answer two different questions:

1. **Does this voice sound AI-generated?**
2. **Does this voice actually belong to the claimed speaker?**

Our solution addresses both.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

We propose a multi-layer AI security pipeline that combines:

### 1. 🎙️ Deepfake / Synthetic Voice Detection
Detects whether the incoming audio is likely to be:

- Authentic human speech
- AI-generated / synthetic speech

### 2. 👤 Speaker Verification
Compares the incoming speaker against the registered/claimed identity and generates a speaker similarity score.

### 3. ⚠️ Risk Engine
Combines the outputs from the deepfake detector and speaker verification system to calculate an overall **risk score** and classify the interaction.

### 4. 🛡️ Prevention Layer
Based on the risk level, the system can:

- Allow the interaction
- Request additional verification
- Flag suspicious activity
- Generate security alerts
- Block high-risk interactions

### 5. 📊 Security Dashboard
Provides a centralized view of:

- Detection results
- Speaker verification
- Risk score
- Threat level
- Security actions
- Alerts and suspicious events
